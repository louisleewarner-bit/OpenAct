Privacy

OpenAct collects nothing. It has no account, analytics, advertising, cookies or tracking, and it sends no information to anyone.

What is stored, and where: your scripts, your settings (theme, panel sizes), and any formats you import are kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. The installed app also keeps a copy of itself so that it works offline. Nothing else is kept, and none of it leaves your device unless you export a file and share it yourself.

Links: "Find a format" and the About screen contain links to other organisations' own pages. They open in your browser only when you choose them, and those sites have their own privacy practices.

Your files: when you import a template, the file is read on your device and is not uploaded.

If someone else hosts OpenAct for you (for example a school or a company), their web server may keep ordinary logs such as your address and the time of a visit. That is the host's practice, not OpenAct's, and they should have their own notice.

Questions and problems: use the issue tracker on the project's repository page.

To remove everything OpenAct has stored, clear this site's data in your browser's settings, or uninstall the app.
