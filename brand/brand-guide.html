OpenAct brand guide

How the name and logo look and are used. Everything here is made by brand/build-brand.py, from one source.

The idea

The mark is a spike mark: the L of tape a stage manager lays on the floor to show where something goes. Two strips, the lower one a little deeper, so the overlap reads. The name is set in Courier Prime Bold, the face of the script page, with the mark standing on its baseline.

Logo

Use the blue lockup on light backgrounds, the light-blue one on dark, and the white or ink one when only one colour is possible. Clear space: keep one mark's width free all round. Smallest size: the lockup 80 px wide on screen, the mark alone 12 px.

App icon

The icon is a script page with tape laid across its corner, lit from the top left, on an ink tile. At 48 px and below it drops the page and keeps the tape. The round one shows the maskable version, which keeps everything inside the safe area.

Contrast: WCAG asks for 4.5 to 1 for text and 3 to 1 for graphics. Blue is for what is current and the one main action; it is never decoration.

Depth

One light, from the top left. Shade is blue-tinted ink, never grey or black. Four levels, each with a meaning: 1 a raised control, 2 a menu or pop-up, 3 a dialog or sheet, and the page, the only large raised surface. In dark mode shadows barely show, so a surface lifts and gets a faint top edge instead.

Type

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for the app, chosen for legibility. Courier Prime for the script page and the name, because the format demands it. Both are under the SIL Open Font License and ship with the app. Four interface sizes (12, 13, 14, 16 px), two weights (400, 600), sentence case, no all-capital labels.

Please don’t

Stretch it
Recolour it
Add shadows or effects
Put it on a busy background

Using the name and logo

  • The OpenAct software is free, under the GNU GPL. The name and logo are not covered by that licence.
  • You may use them to say that you wrote or exported a script with OpenAct, or to link to it.
  • If you change the software and share your version, give it another name and logo, so no one mistakes it for OpenAct.
  • Write the name as one word, OpenAct, never “Act-One” or “Act One”.
  • No trade mark has been filed or cleared yet (docs/research/names.md). This page is guidance, not legal advice.
