# Name shortlist

## Decision: ActOne (2026-11-08)
The owner chose **ActOne** (D-029), written as one word. Checked the same day:
- **No script-writing app** called ActOne or Act One turned up in app store and web searches.
- **No plain ACT ONE or ACTONE software mark** appeared on US trade mark aggregators. Near matches: **ACTONE GOVERNMENT SOLUTIONS**, registered in class 42 for workforce software ([Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/881/88/actone-government-88188461.html)); ACT-ON, a marketing-software mark ([Justia](https://trademarks.justia.com/863/20/act-86320077.html)); 1 ACT ONE CREATIVE, cancelled ([Trademarkia](https://www.trademarkia.com/1-act-one-creative-llc-78611712)).
- **Runway's Act-One** is a well-known AI tool that animates film characters from an actor's video ([Runway](https://runwayml.com/research/introducing-act-one), [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2024/10/24/runways-mindblowing-act-one-transforms-an-actor-into-a-cartoon-from-just-one-video)). It is the nearest risk: the same broad field, and an AI association.
- **"Act One" is a script heading**, so the name is weaker as a mark and competes with ordinary search results.

What to do: always write "ActOne", one word; pair it with a descriptor ("ActOne, the free script editor for stage and screen"); search the UK IPO, EUIPO and USPTO registers in classes 9 and 42; file a UK mark if clear; have the launch solicitor confirm.

The research below is the record of how the shortlist was built.

## Shortlist research

"Cue", the working title, was crowded: the App Store alone has several apps called Cue, mostly teleprompters for actors and creators, and a theatre cueing app ([Cold read & Teleprompter: Cue](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6748885181), [Cue: Floating Teleprompter](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6758642341), [Cue: Script Reader](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6744923182), [Cue to Cue Theatre Edition](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1291690309)). Several sit in the same space as a script editor, which is the worst kind of clash (legal review L5).

Checked 2026-11-08 with web searches of app stores and the web, plus US trade mark listings on aggregator sites (Justia, Trademarkia, uspto.report). The official registers (UK IPO, USPTO, TMview) couldn't be reached from the sandbox, so none of this replaces a register search (below).

## What a good name needs
- Says scripts, theatre or the page, across screen, stage, audio and comics, without being a generic word.
- Short, easy to say and spell in British and American English.
- No AI connotation (writers distrust it; competitors.md S29).
- Clear of other writing, script and theatre software, so it can be registered and found.

## Recommendation at the time
**Matinee** first: it is the warmest, says theatre and film at once, and the searches found no software use or live software mark. **Scenebook** is the plainest alternative and works for every format. **Pagelight** and **Inkstage** are coined, so they are the easiest to register and to get domains for.

## Before choosing
1. Search the registers in classes 9 (software) and 42 (software services): [UK IPO](https://trademarks.ipo.gov.uk/ipo-tmtext), [TMview](https://www.tmdn.org/tmview/) (EU and national offices) and the [USPTO](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/).
2. Search the App Store, Google Play, GitHub, npm and the domains you'd want (for example the .app and .co.uk names).
3. Say the name aloud to three writers; a name has to survive being said in a rehearsal room.
4. Ask the solicitor reviewing the launch (legal review, action 6) to confirm the choice; consider filing a UK mark.
5. Log the decision in decisions.md, then rename: CLAUDE.md, the prototype's wordmark, the manifest and the overview page.
