# Positioning

## Statement
For UK stage writers (panto, musical and play writers, and the amateur companies they write for) and for screenwriters who want professional pages without paying, OpenAct is a free, local-first script-writing app that gets the format right for stage and screen. Unlike screenwriting apps that cap free scripts, watermark exports or treat stage plays as an afterthought, OpenAct handles UK and US stage layouts, songs and panto structure natively, exports clean A4 or US Letter PDF and Word files, and keeps every script on the writer's device in open formats.

## Where OpenAct sits
Two axes show the gap: **price model** (paid or capped, through to free and unlimited) and **scope** (screenplay only, through to stage and screen). Paid tools (Final Draft, Scrivener, Story Architect's paid tiers) reach some way into stage. Free, unlimited tools (Trelby, Beat, Afterwriting) are screenplay-only and tied to one platform. The free, stage-and-screen, any-device corner is empty.

## Opportunities
- Own "stage and screen, free" as a position: no competitor claims it.
- Pantomime is a niche with no tool at all, and an annual writing cycle (most pantos open in December).
- Accurate pages are the proof point: a free tool whose PDFs match Final Draft's page breaks undercuts the main reason to pay.
- Privacy and no AI by default answer a visible worry.

## Threats
- WriterDuet or Arc Studio could lift their free caps; Beat could ship beyond the Mac. Parity on "free screenwriting" is not a moat; the stage, music and panto depth is.
- Story Architect already spans formats (stage play, audio drama) and is open source.
- Writers may stay in Word because directors want Word. That's why DOCX export is in the MVP.
- AI-led newcomers may pull beginners. OpenAct's answer is format quality and ownership, not matching AI features.

## Strategic implications
- **Differentiate on:** stage layouts, songs and lyrics, panto structure, casting helpers, A4 and Word output.
- **Match others on:** screenplay formatting and pagination, Fountain and FDX in and out, PDF output.
- **Leave out for now:** production scheduling and budgeting, live co-writing, AI.
- **Monitor:** competitors' free-tier limits, Beat's platforms, Story Architect's stage features, new web-based entrants.
